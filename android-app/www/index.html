fisync

Scan the code on your computer, or enter the address and PIN. Then click Allow there. For USB, plug in the phone and choose "Connect a phone over USB" in the fisync menu on the computer.

Android may stop fisync in the background. Turn off battery restrictions for fisync to keep the link up.

fisync runs in the background and shows a notification while it is connected. Calls, texts and photos keep working when this screen is closed.

Works only when the computer is on the same Wi-Fi and allows Wake-on-LAN.

Settings

To hear calls on the computer, pair the computer with this phone over Bluetooth and allow it to handle calls. Then switch call audio to Bluetooth from the phone's call screen.

Quick actions

Notifications

To send other apps' notifications to the computer, allow fisync to read notifications.

Crash report

Stored only on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
