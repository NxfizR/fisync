# Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026

fisync lets you control your computer from your phone and send files between your own devices over your local network. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

**In short:** fisync does not collect your data. There is no account, no server run by me, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays between your own devices.

## What fisync handles

- **Files you send or receive.** They are saved in a folder named `fisync` in your home folder on the computer, and stay there until you delete them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.
- **Mouse, keyboard and typed text.** Sent from your phone to your computer and carried out there. They are not stored or logged.
- **Calls, messages and photos (Android app).** While the app is connected, your text messages, call log (numbers, names, times) and call events are read from your phone and shown on your computer. They pass through the computer's memory and are not saved. Photos you sync are copied into the `fisync` folder on the computer. When you ask the computer to place or answer a call or send a text, your phone does it; the call and its audio stay on your phone.
- **The PIN.** A random 6-digit PIN is created each time the computer app starts. It is held in memory and disappears when the app closes. Your phone's browser keeps it only for the current browsing session.
- **Failed PIN attempts.** To limit guessing, the computer remembers the network address of a device that enters a wrong PIN, in memory only, for about a minute. It is discarded when the app closes.
- **Last address (Android app).** The Android app remembers the computer address you last typed, on your phone only. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove it.

## What I collect

Nothing. I do not receive or have access to your files, input, PIN, network address or how you use the app.

## Permissions

The Android app asks for permission to read and send text messages, read your call log and call state, place and answer calls, and read your photos. It uses them only for the features described above. You can refuse any of them in Android settings, which turns off the matching feature. It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Local network and encryption

fisync talks over plain HTTP on your local network. Traffic, including the PIN, is not encrypted in transit, so someone else on the same network could in principle watch or interfere with it. Use fisync on networks you trust, not on public Wi-Fi.

## Third parties

fisync does not send data to any third party and contains no analytics or advertising code. If you download fisync from GitHub, GitHub's own policies apply to that download.

## Children

fisync is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone.

## Your control

Delete the files in the `fisync` folder at any time. Close the app to clear the PIN. Uninstall it to remove everything it stored. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to export or erase.
